    Test setups

    How you test: drop height and surface. Pick one and keep using it, so tests compare. A setup can't be changed once used, except its mid mark: the second mark on the wall that the video app calibrates with, near typical rebound heights.

      New-ball reference values

      Like-new compares against new balls tested the same way: a batch's own new-ball tests, then other batches of the same brand and model. Enter a value here for balls you never tested new. To set one for a single batch, edit the batch.

        ITF conversion

        Only needed when your setup isn't the ITF method. On one day, drop about 10 balls, from new to flat, both your way and from 100 in onto concrete. Enter one pair per ball.

          Charge session

          A session is the balls' whole stay in a canister: start it when they go in and it's pumped up, end it when they come out to play. Lowering it to maintenance pressure isn't an end: the app sees that from the readings.

          Log play

          With the hours, the app can learn what an hour of play costs these balls. A bounce test before the balls go back in a canister tells it most.
