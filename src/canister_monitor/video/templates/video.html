    How to film a test

    1. Stand the camera somewhere steady, 2–3 m from the drop line, with the whole drop and the floor in view.
    2. Start with three holds of about 2 seconds: the ball still on the floor, at the mid mark, then at the drop mark.
    3. Drop each ball in number order, the same number of times, and catch it after its first bounce.
